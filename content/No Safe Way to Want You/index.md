# Overview

>**Some people are easy to admire. Fewer are safe to be wanted by.**
>
>Earth, around 2080. Military enhancement has created a small class of soldiers with extraordinary strength, while governments, corporations, and the armed forces decide who gets access and who pays for it.
>
>Sergeant Ranelle Spade is a gifted communications specialist who never asked for this life. Introverted, creative, and unenhanced, they keep their head down and their enthusiasms private.
>
>Staff Sergeant Aeirith Voeren is an enhanced special-operations soldier whose strength stands out even among her peers. She is confident in battle and often treated as a weapon or a threat rather than a person.
>
>When an operation goes wrong and each of them is forced to save the other, a distant attraction becomes something personal. Neither is prepared for how badly they will begin wanting more.
>
>**What do you owe the person who wants you, and what do they owe you in return?**
