# Non-Canon AU Stories

> [!warning] Not canon
> Everything in this section is **alternate-universe fanfiction**. Events, relationships, and characterization here do not establish canon for *Torn Usunder*. Some stories may contradict the main storyline on purpose.

These stories are shared for friends to read. Each one has its own summary below, along with where it branches from canon.

## Story Index

### [[Hollow Hours]]
**Status:** Complete
**Setting:** Near the end of Act 2
**Content notes:** Mature. Contains strong language, emotional distress, themes of guilt and self-hatred, and sexual content.

**Summary:**
After a mission, Bloof shuts themself in a dark room in the sanctuary. They can't stop hearing what their enemy said: that Bloof was a monster who took someone away from them. Bazemir finds them and refuses to let them carry it alone. He tells Bloof about the hunting of his people and the cold years he spent frozen, and what that taught him about grief. Bloof breaks down, and the night turns into a confession of feelings neither of them has said aloud. 

**Non-canon elements:**
- The romance between Bloof and Bazemir
- Bazemir's softened stance toward Bloof
- The cause of the enemy's grief
