# Cornered

>[!note] Character pronouns
>Bloof uses they/them, and Seris uses she/her


The music was the first problem. The second problem was the number of beastfolk who wanted to shake Bloof's hand.

Bloof stood just inside the doorway of the rented hall, in baggy techwear cargos and a black compression tank that left their arms bare. Every scar on them was on display. Their tail was pinned tight against the back of one leg, and the tip of it twitched with each new voice. _Five minutes,_ they had promised themself. _Be seen, then leave._

"Bloovrn," a wolfish guy said, pumping their hand. "Seris talks about you all the time!"

"Does she," Bloof said flatly. Their ears flicked back. "That's... concerning."

Every conversation felt like a puzzle with the instructions missing. Someone asked what they studied, and Bloof managed a few honest sentences about magi-tech hardware. Then three people started talking over each other, the bass climbed, and the room got too bright and too crowded. The sharp edge of overload began to creep in behind Bloof's eyes. They felt the faint pressure of the Crown Eyes straining against their closed lids.

So they found the bar.

The first one was to steady their hands. The second was because it worked. By the third, the room had softened at the edges, and their tail had loosened enough to sway behind them. The fourth? Well, that was just for fun. Bloof ended up against a wall near the back, finishing the glass and watching the crowd move.

_This is Seris's fault,_ they thought, with something that wasn't quite resentment. _"Just one hour, Bloof. You'll survive."_

---

Seris was **not** having the same night.

She moved through the party like it had been built around her. White coat tails swept behind her, the pearls around her neck caught the light, and her purple tail rode lazily at her heels. Every few steps someone tried to get her attention, and she gave each of them exactly as much as she meant to. Tonight she had more than usual. A glass of something amber was already half gone in her clawed hand, and her smile came easier than it normally did.

She found Bloof by tracking the one spot in the room where the crowd thinned out. They were against the far wall, tipping back the last of a drink, with their ears drooping and their tail swaying in a slow, unguarded arc.

Seris stopped a few steps away and let her smile widen until the fangs showed.

"Well, well," she said, low and smooth. "The famous Bloof Graymark. Hiding by the wall like a cornered cat."

Bloof lowered the glass and looked at her. Their upper eyelids twitched but stayed shut. "Dragon-goat, technically," they said. "And I'm not hiding. I'm observing from a defensible position."

"Mm. Defending yourself from what? Fun?" She came closer and plucked the empty glass from their fingers to sniff it. "Three drinks. Maybe four. You're swaying, darling."

"I'm _standing,_" Bloof said, with great dignity. Their tail thumped once against the wall. "*You* peer-pressured me into a room full of strangers. I'm allowed to be a little drunk."

"*A little*?" Her eyes glittered. "I've seen you take a hit from something three times your size without blinking, and a crowded party does this to you."

"Crowds are worse than hits." Bloof's mouth twitched into a small, crooked smile. "Hits, I understand."

Seris laughed, a low, genuine sound. Her tail curled in a slow, pleased loop behind her.

"Come on," she said, tipping her head toward a side door. "You've been brave enough for one night. Want a break from all the fur and noise? I have a room upstairs."

Bloof's tail went still for half a second. Then it swung out behind them, wide and unmistakably relieved.

"ABSOLUTELY," they said, much too quickly. "*ahem*, yes. Please."

---

The suite was quiet in a way that felt like a held breath. Seris locked the door, and the party shrank to a dull heartbeat through the floor.

Bloof exhaled all at once. Their shoulders dropped, their ears came up, and for the first time in an hour they breathed all the way down. Their tail unwound from their leg and swayed behind them, loose and easy.

"There it is," Seris murmured. She poured two drinks from a bottle on the sideboard, and before she handed one over she tilted hers and studied it, a habit so old she did it without thinking. "You look like a different person when no one's watching."

"I'm not different. Just _less overwhelmed._" Bloof took the glass and sank onto the arm of the couch, close to the wall. "Thanks. Really. Even if you did drag me here."

"I _invited_ you."

"You sent me eleven messages."

"Persuasive invitations."

"One of them was just a photo of your face," Bloof said, side-eying her. "Smirking."

"It worked, didn't it?" Seris leaned against the sideboard. Her tail flicked, a lazy, smug twitch. "You're here."

"I'm here," Bloof agreed, letting out an audible sigh. A little of the alcohol warmed their voice, and they let the smile through. "I'd be mad, but you've got the better snacks and the quieter room. Suspiciously well-planned, honestly. Did you plan this? Leave me out there to melt, then rescue me with a suite?"

Her eyebrow arched. "You think I'm that *devious*?"

"I think you're exactly that devious." Bloof sipped, watching her over the rim. "You walked up to me like a predator that already had the prey cornered. Very dramatic. Great coat, by the way. Very '*villain in the second act*.'"

For half a second Seris's composure slipped, and her tail jerked in surprise. Then the smile came back, slower and warmer.

"Careful," she said softly. "Flattery will get you everywhere."

"I said _villain._"

"I heard 'great coat.'"

Bloof laughed, a real one that shook their shoulders. Seris watched it with an expression she kept carefully light, and her tail slid slowly along the floor toward them.

---

Time slipped by in easy pieces. They traded dumb stories, and she teased them about their cargo pants. They fired back that her tail had been tapping her ankle the whole time she called herself _composed._ She went still and looked down. Bloof grinned in triumph.

"Lucky guess," she said.

"See, I notice things too," Bloof said. "You just don't think I do."

She tilted her head, and for a moment the playful look turned thoughtful. Then she set down her drink and wandered over, close enough that her tail brushed the couch.

"It's a wonder, honestly," Seris said, "that no one's snapped you up. Look at you. Smart. Dangerous. Those _arms._ And you're still single."

The words landed somewhere unexpected. Bloof's ears twitched, and their tail stiffened and curled in on itself.

"Ha. Yeah. Well." They stood up, a little too quickly, and ended up with their back against the wall. "Not really the priority. School, work, jobs, my mom. You know."

"I know what you _say._" She took a step. "That's not the same as a reason."

"It's a pretty good reason, four, actually." They scratched at the back of their neck, avoiding her eyes. "Can we talk about something else? Like how you were so sure you could get me to come tonight?"

"Hm." Another step. Her tail slid behind her, slow and curious. "No."

Bloof's ears pinned halfway back. "Seris."

"You went quiet. Your tail did that thing." She smiled, small and sharp. "Interesting."

"It's _not_ interesting." Bloof set their glass on a nearby table with exaggerated care, as if that would restore their dignity. "I'm going back to the party. People are probably wondering where we went."

"Of course," Seris said sweetly, though unmoving.

Bloof moved to leave. They got two steps, and then a hand was flat against the wall beside their head. The whole world narrowed to the sound of Seris's coat tails settling, the smell of something faintly floral and sharp, and the cold weight of her tail curling around Bloof's ankle.

Bloof breathing ceased.

Seris stood close, with six feet and two inches of lean, easy confidence pinning them against the plaster. She didn't touch them with anything more than that one hand, and the grin on her face was entirely cocky.

"You really want to go back out there?" she asked, though both knew the answer. The question was as sharp as her fangs, and aimed precisely at her target.

Heat climbed Bloof's neck and ears, and their tail snapped taut against the floor. The Crown Eyes twitched hard under the lids, wanting to open, and Bloof clamped down on them. Their face went carefully, stubbornly blank. Seris saw all of it anyway.

"What," Bloof said evenly, "are you doing?"

"Hmm." She leaned in until her breath brushed the base of their horn. "I'm answering your question. The one you didn't ask."

"I asked a _different_ one."

"You know, you're not very good at *stoicism* when you're drunk." Her clawed fingertip trailed down the dark line of their forearm, over the old scars, and her touch was light and unhurried. "Your ears give you away. And this." Her tail gave the gentlest tug at their ankle, and Bloof's own tail flailed once, a pure betrayal. "Every part of you is telling me something very different from your face."

"That's... a lot of confidence for someone who just got stared down by a dragon." Bloof's eyes narrowed, staring directly into Seris'. 

"Oh, darling." She laughed under her breath, the sound dropping lower. "I've been _wanting_ to see you try that voice on me. *Say it again*."

Bloof's jaw worked. "I'm not saying anything."

"Mm. That's too bad." Her thumb traced the edge of the tank's short sleeve, just below the shoulder. "I had a few things I wanted to say to you. Things I've been _holding back_ all night, in a room full of beastkin."

"Seris..."

"Do you want to hear them?" Her voice dipped, velvet and wicked. "Or do you want to go back downstairs and pretend you didn't just stop breathing?"

Bloof opened their mouth. Nothing came out. Their tail curled halfway round Seris's, the way a person might grab a rope, and neither of them said a word about it.

---

Neither of them spoke.

The party thudded faintly through the floor, and the only sound in the suite was Bloof's breathing, which had gone shallow and too deliberate. Seris held her pose, one hand braced beside their horn and her tail still looped around their ankle. She watched them with the patience of someone who had already won and wanted to see how long the other side would take to notice.

Bloof decided not to notice.

They shifted their weight, a slow tilt of the shoulder, and pushed their free hand flat against her collarbone. It wasn't a shove, just a firm pressure that said _back up._ Their claws rested against the white of her coat, silver against silk. If they could turn the angle, they could slip out sideways and reclaim the space.

Seris let the hand sit there for a heartbeat. Then she turned her head and brushed her lips against the inside of their wrist, a ghost of contact, fangs just barely visible.

Bloof's fingers twitched, and the pressure faltered.

_Okay,_ they thought, with the clarity of four drinks. _Adjust._

They dropped their other hand to catch her hip, planning to hook a leg behind hers and pivot. It was a clean, practiced move, the kind Bloof had used in sparring on people twice Seris's weight. Their tail even came off the floor to counterbalance.

Seris's tail tightened around their ankle with a slow, deliberate squeeze, and Bloof's weight didn't go anywhere. Her hand slid from the wall to catch their wrist and press it gently, firmly back against the plaster above their head. She didn't use force. It was just exactly enough, like she had measured it.

Bloof tried again with the other hand. She caught that one and pinned it too.

_Oh._

Their ears slicked back and their breath caught. Their tail whipped once against the floor, loud in the silence, and neither of them looked down at it. The Crown Eyes strained against their lids, and for half a second a thin seam of red light showed beneath the left one before Bloof forced it shut.

Seris saw it. Her smile grew into something slow and pleased.

_She planned this,_ Bloof realized. _The couch, the wall, the "wonder you're single." All of it._

She still hadn't said a word. She let them stand there, pinned, with their pulse hammering against her fingertips and the heat climbing up past their jaw, over the star on their forehead, to the tips of their ears. There was nothing left to hide behind. The flush ran bright under the grey of their scales, visible even in the low light.

Seris's eyes dropped to it and lingered, appearing almost...*hungry*.

Her head dipped, slowly, and she turned her face toward the side of their neck. She stopped a finger's width from the skin, and the warmth of her breath rolled down the curve of it, long and deliberate.

Bloof's whole body jolted. Their tail locked rigid, then curled inward like it wanted to hide.

She breathed out again, even slower, and the corner of her mouth curved against the edge of their jaw.

Then she opened her mouth and bit their ear.

It was gentle, just the point of a fang and the pressure of her lips against the soft fur and the cold metal of the bar piercing. A low sound escaped Bloof before they could stop it, small and startled and unmistakably not a word.

Seris went still against them, and her tail gave a slow, delighted flick.

---

"_Shut up,_" Bloof said hoarsely, though neither of them had said anything.

Seris pulled back just far enough to meet their eyes. They were cyan and gleaming, with all the dry amusement in the world. "I haven't said a word, darling."

"You were _thinking_ it."

"I was thinking," she said, silky, "that you made a very interesting noise."

"I didn't." Bloof's voice cracked on the second word. They cleared their throat, and their ears flattened in sheer embarrassment. "That was a... hiccup. Alcohol-related."

"Ah, alcohol-related." Her thumb stroked slowly over the inside of their wrist. "Of course."

"Don't do the voice."

"What voice?"

"The _one_ you're doing." Bloof tried to shrug one shoulder, a casual gesture ruined entirely by the fact that they were still pinned. "The villain-monologue one. '*Oh, I've been planning this for weeks.*' I'm not impressed."

"Aren't you?" Her tail slid higher, a lazy coil around their calf. "Your tail seems quite impressed."

"My tail is _drunk._"

"Your tail is honest." She tilted her head, the smile razor-thin. "That's the difference between you and it."

"I'm honest!" Bloof said, a little too loudly. "I'm extremely honest. I'm the most honest person you know."

"You told me you weren't hiding by that wall."

"I was observing."

"With your tail around your leg."

"_Tactically._"

Seris laughed against their cheek, low and warm, and the sound ran through Bloof like a current. They squeezed their eyes shut, which only made their hands feel more trapped. They dragged them open again and glared at her with all the stoicism they had left.

"This is a power play," they said. "I see what you're doing. You think because I'm a little off-balance—"

"A little."

"—_a little_ off-balance, I'll just fold. But I've been in worse spots than this. I've been shot at. I've been—"

She leaned in and licked their neck.

Bloof's sentence ended in a soft, strangled noise.

"You were saying?" Seris murmured, lips so close to their pulse that they could feel her smile.

"I was saying," Bloof managed, "that you're, um, very. Confident."

"And you're very flustered."

"I'm _composed._"

"You're purple to the horns, darling."

"I'm _warm._ The room is warm. It's genetic."

"Oh, absolutely," she agreed gravely. "I'll have it looked at." Her thumb pressed the pulse point of their wrist, and she felt it jump. "After."

"After _what?_"

She tilted her head, studying them with open, wicked interest, like a predator who's finally cornered their prey.

"You tell me."

Bloof opened their mouth to snap back, but nothing sharp came to mind. Their tail betrayed them again by sliding slowly, helplessly, around hers.

"...You're insufferable," they muttered.

"You're adorable."

"I'm not _adorable._ I could set this room on fire."

"You could." Her grin widened, all fang. "You won't."

"I _could._"

"Then do it, Bloof." She leaned in until her nose brushed theirs, and her voice dropped to almost nothing. "*Make me stop*."

Neither of them moved, and the room was very, very quiet.

---
"I'm not saying anything," Bloof repeated, a little hoarse.

"You keep saying that." Seris's thumb slid up the edge of their sleeve, over the bare curve of their shoulder, and settled at the hinge of their jaw where the scales gave way to soft fur. "And yet you haven't left."

"The wall's in the way", the red in their face still betraying them, held back only by willpower and spite, yet contested by embarrassment and liquor.

"The wall isn't doing anything. _I'm_ the one in the way." Her smile sharpened. "Push me, then. You could. We both know it."

Bloof's eyes dropped to her mouth and rose again, too slowly to pass for casual. Their tail tightened around her tail and held on.

"...Annoying," they muttered.

"Mm?"

"You're _really_ annoying, you know that?" Their voice cracked on the last word and they hated it. "You do this on purpose. You always do this on purpose. Gorgeous, smug, and sitting in my head rent-free for months, and you just..."

They stopped. Seris had gone completely still.

"*Gorgeous*," she repeated softly.

Bloof's ears flattened. "I didn't say that."

"You did."

"It was the alcohol."

"It was _absolutely_ the alcohol, and I'm going to remember it forever." But her tail gave a quick, embarrassed little flick against the floor, and Bloof caught it. Their grin came back, warm and crooked.

"You're blushing," they said. "Under the purple. It's faint, but it's there."

"I do not *blush*."

"Your ears... well, you don't have ears. Your _neck._ Your neck is doing it." They lifted a hand and ran one silver claw, very gently, along the glowing cyan markings at her throat. Seris's breath caught audibly. "Oh, that's _really_ good. I'm keeping that."

"*Bloof*." A warning, but her voice came out too low to carry it.

"Say please."

Her eyes narrowed. Then she laughed under her breath, dangerous and delighted, and caught their wrist against the wall.

"Oh, you _brat,_" she murmured. "You're going to regret that."

"Doubt it."

She kissed them.

It wasn't gentle. Months of teasing and near-misses went into it, and Bloof made a startled sound against her mouth before their hands fisted in the white lapels of her coat and hauled her closer. Fangs, heat, the taste of whatever she'd been drinking. It was *delicious*. Seris's tail wound up their leg, past the cargo pocket and the knee, tight enough to make Bloof's breath stutter. Bloof's own tail lashed once against the wall, then wrapped around her waist like it had always belonged there.

When they broke apart, both of them were breathing hard.

"That," Bloof managed, "was... not nothing."

"No." Seris's voice had lost most of its polish. "It wasn't."

"Bed?" Bloof said, and then, appalled, "Gods, I said that out loud."

"You did." Her grin turned wolfish. "Good instincts."

---

They didn't make it gracefully.

Seris walked them backward with a hand at their hip, and Bloof walked her back, and somewhere along the way they swapped places twice. Her coat ended up half off one shoulder. The pearl at her throat swung against Bloof's cheek when they kissed her neck, and she hissed through her fangs and arched into it before she could stop herself.

"Don't," she said.

"Don't what?" Bloof's mouth moved lower, pressing against the pulse beneath the glowing scales. "Don't do _this?_"

"Ah..." Her claws dug into their back. "Bloof..."

"That's the second time you've said my name like that." They looked up, flushed and smug and wrecked. "I'm keeping that one too."

Seris's answer was to shove them back onto the enormous bed, and she followed. The mattress swallowed them both. She straddled their hips, loomed over them with her hair falling loose around her horns, and looked down at them with an expression halfway between hunger and fondness.

"You think you're winning," she said.

"I _know_ I'm winning." But their hands had gone to her hips, and their voice was hardly steady. "Your tail's shaking."

"That's anticipation."

"Is that what we're calling it?"

She bent and caught their bottom lip between her teeth, carefully, and Bloof's whole body shivered. Her hands slid beneath the hem of the compression tank, cool claws against hot scales, and pushed it up. The air touched their stomach, and the muscle there jumped. Her gaze tracked every old scar like it was something worth keeping.

"Still with me?" she asked, and for a moment the tease was gone and she meant the question.

"Yes." Bloof's tail curled around her thigh, and they didn't look away. "Very. Don't stop."

Something eased in her face. Then it burned again.

They rolled. Bloof ended up on top, pinning her wrists above her head, grinning like they'd won a war. Their breath came hot against her ear. "Not so composed now, are you?"

"Give me a minute," Seris panted, "and I'll fix that."

"Mm. I'll give you thirty seconds."

They kissed her again, slowly this time, unhurried and thorough, and their hips shifted in a way that wrenched a sharp, helpless sound from her throat. Her tail slammed against the sheets. It was the first time all night she'd lost the thread of what she wanted to say, and Bloof took a ridiculous amount of glee in it, nosing at the soft place under her jaw while she swore at them in a language they didn't recognize.

"Oh. Now that's new," Bloof murmured. "What does that one mean?"

"It means," she gasped, "that you are _enjoying this too much._"

"Oh, *absolutely* I am."

---

She turned it around with a single move.

One second Bloof was winning. The next, Seris had slipped her wrists free, flipped them, and had them pinned face-down against the mattress with a knee settled between their legs and her weight behind it. A clawed hand pulled their tank top high, exposing the long line of their back.

"Seris..."

"Shh." Her voice had dropped back into that smooth, low register, with a smile in it Bloof could hear. "You had your turn."

Her fingertips found the ridge of scales along their spine and traced down it, slow as a held breath. Bloof's whole body arched. A shudder rolled down to the base of their tail, which lashed wildly, once, then went rigid.

"There," Seris whispered. "That's one."

Bloof tried to answer. It escaped as a tight, breathless noise.

She leaned down and kissed the back of their neck, right at the base of one swept-back horn. Then she did something else. She raked a claw slowly up the line of their spine, rubbed her thumb along the thick ridge where the horn met skull, and pressed.

The sound that came out of Bloof was nothing like their usual voice. It started low in their chest, a deep, helpless rumble like a furnace banking, and went on and on. Their eyes fluttered shut. Their ears fell soft and flat. Their tail went loose and happy against her leg, and Bloof couldn't have told you whether they were still breathing.

Seris froze, then smiled, slow and entirely satisfied.

"Ohhhhh," she murmured. "_There_ it is."

"Don't," Bloof tried. It came out as a purr. "Don't... stop..."

"I wasn't planning to."

The fight went out of them all at once, every ounce of stubbornness gone. They melted into the sheets and let her take her time, and she did, mapping every sensitive place she'd found with claws and mouth and patience. Bloof's rumble rose and fell with her touch. Twice they reached back blindly for her, and both times she caught their hand and laced her fingers through theirs.

"Seris," they whispered at last, raw and unguarded. "Please."

Her voice was softer than it had ever been. "Tell me what you want."

They told her. It wasn't elegant. There was a lot of her name in it, and none of the banter was left. The last thing in the room that resembled composure was her tail, wrapped around their waist and holding on like it never meant to let go.

---

Later, the room was quiet. The bass downstairs had faded to a distant pulse.

Bloof lay sprawled on their stomach with their cheek in the pillow, hair wrecked, one horn smudged with a faint cyan glow where her fingertips had been. Their tail was draped loosely over her ankle, and the rumble in their chest hadn't fully died, just dropped to a contented hum. The upper eyes stayed closed. For once they'd had nothing to watch for.

Seris lay propped on one elbow beside them, coat discarded, pearl necklace askew, tracing slow patterns along the scales of their shoulder. Her tail coiled around theirs. She hadn't stopped smiling.

"Well," she said, voice lazy. "Shall we get back to the party?"

Bloof's whole body went rigid.

Their eyes snapped open. The hum cut out. Their tail slapped against the mattress, wildly, and they pushed themself halfway up on one elbow, staring at her with wide, panicked eyes and ears standing straight up.

"The... _what?_ Now? We're... I'm not... my _shirt..._" They looked down at themself, then around the room for pants, then at her again. "Seris, I can't go back down there looking like _this._"

Seris let it hang for exactly three seconds. Then she buried her face in the pillow and shook with laughter.

"You," she wheezed, "should see your _face._"

Bloof stared. Realization crept in. Their ears flattened, their tail whipped, and heat rose all the way to the tips of their horns.

"You absolute _snake._"

"Viper, technically." She wiped her eyes, still grinning. Then she reached over and tugged them back down against her side, tucking their head beneath her chin. Her tail wrapped around theirs again. "I'm not letting you leave for a very long time, darling. The party can survive without us."

Bloof grumbled something into her collarbone. Their tail, traitorous to the last, settled warm and boneless over hers.

The rumble started again, low and steady, and neither of them mentioned it.
