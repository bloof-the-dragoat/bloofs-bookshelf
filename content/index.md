# Welcome

A collection of original fiction and the worldbuilding behind it. Each story has its own setting, tone, and content notice, so check the story page before you start.

---

## The Stories

### [[Torn Usunder/index|Torn Usunder]]
*Dark fantasy. A world of beastfolk, buried dragons, and seven forces that were never allowed to be gentle.*

Bloof is a dragon-goat hybrid with a past they've buried, in a world that already distrusts dragon blood. Then the leylines begin to stir.

### [[No Safe Way to Want You/index|No Safe Way to Want You]]
*Near-future dark romance. A story about being wanted, being needed, and the dangerous space between them.*

Earth, around 2080. After a disastrous operation forces two soldiers to save each other, distant attraction becomes something personal.
