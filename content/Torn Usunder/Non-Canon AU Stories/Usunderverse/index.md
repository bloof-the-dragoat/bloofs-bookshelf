# Usunderverse

> Bloof wanted rest.
> Hell had other plans.

One moment, Bloof is asleep beside someone they trust. The next, they’re bleeding onto unfamiliar pavement beneath a red sky, their phone won’t connect, and every stranger seems to have an opinion about their horns.

They need food. Somewhere safe. A way home.

Hell is offering none of those without complications.

Here, violence is background noise, kindness deserves a second look, and looking important can be considerably more dangerous than looking lost. Bloof has survived worse than a hostile street—but they’ve never had to do it somewhere that makes the power beneath their ribs feel quite so awake.

And then there’s **Vendetta**.

A demonic shark with a sharp mind, sharper teeth, and absolutely no patience for anything that looks like royalty. Bloof would very much like to be left alone. Vendetta has already decided that’s an attitude problem.

Their first impressions could use some work.

Welcome to *Usunderverse*: a character-driven crossover between *Torn Usunder* and the world of *Helluva Boss* and *Hazbin Hotel*. Expect fraying composure, spectacularly bad assumptions, uneasy kindness, and the difficult business of deciding who to trust when home is somewhere nobody has heard of.

Getting through Hell is one problem.

Getting along might be worse.

## About this crossover

*Usunderverse* is a non-canon AU featuring Bloof, Vendetta, and familiar faces from the Hellaverse.

Set roughly a century after the main events of *Helluva Boss*, this version of Hell has its own history, changed relationships, and room for new trouble. No prior knowledge of *Torn Usunder* is required to meet Bloof at their worst possible arrival time.
