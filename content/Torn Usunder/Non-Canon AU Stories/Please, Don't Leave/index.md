# Please, Don't Leave

> For once, Wrath is quiet.
> Something in the woods isn't.

For one foolish, wonderful breath, Bloof thinks they might be allowed to enjoy the silence.

No familiar pressure beneath their ribs. No answering weight when they reach inward. Just cold air, damp earth, and trees they don't recognize.

Then a voice asks them to come back.

Bloof is already running.

Something moves through the forest faster than it should. Their Crown Eyes cannot find it. Fire buys distance, but never enough, and the trees seem determined to lead them somewhere they have already been.

The voice keeps calling.

It sounds frightened.

That does not make it safe.

Welcome to *Please, Don't Leave*: a short, character-driven horror AU about unfamiliar woods, uncertain intentions, and the difficult distinction between being wanted and being allowed to leave.

Somewhere in the darkness, something wants company.

Bloof would prefer an introduction first.

## About this story

*Please, Don't Leave* is a non-canon *Torn Usunder* AU that places Bloof in a different world, without their equipment or access to Wrath.

Planned as a compact, five-chapter story.

No prior knowledge of *Torn Usunder* is required. What matters at the beginning is straightforward: Bloof is lost, something is following them, and it would very much like them to stop running.

---

## Continuity

This story stands apart from main-story *Torn Usunder* and other AUs.

Its setting, supernatural conditions, and events belong to this continuity. Familiar abilities may interact differently with this world without establishing new rules for the main story.
