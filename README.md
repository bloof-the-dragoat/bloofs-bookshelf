# Bloof's Bookshelf

> *Stories of found family, dangerous power, and the people caught inside both.*

Bloof's Bookshelf is a collection of original fiction and the worldbuilding behind it. This repository contains the source for the site, including story chapters, character profiles, setting guides, planning notes, and alternate-universe stories.

Each project has its own setting, tone, continuity, and content notices. Some pages are written for readers; others offer a look behind the scenes at stories still taking shape.

> 🚧 **Work in progress:** Stories and supporting notes are at different stages of development. Drafts, outlines, and worldbuilding may change as the projects grow.

---

## The Stories

### Torn Usunder

*Dark fantasy. A world of beastfolk, buried dragons, and seven forces that were never allowed to be gentle.*

Bloovrn Graymark—usually called Bloof—is a dragon-goat hybrid who escaped a laboratory as a child. Made the host of Wrath, one of seven world-altering Calamities, they are trying to build an ordinary life in a world that already distrusts dragon blood.

Then the ancient leylines begin to stir, drawing the attention of gods, Apostles, corporations, and factions searching for the Calamity hosts.

A story about personhood, dangerous power, found family, and whether the thing done to you has to decide who you become.

### No Safe Way to Want You

*Near-future dark romance. A story about being wanted, being needed, and the dangerous space between them.*

Earth, around 2080. After a disastrous operation forces two soldiers to save each other, distant attraction becomes something personal.

Ranelle Spade and Aeirith Voeren find connection through shared danger, unexpected tenderness, and the experience of being seen. But connection does not make either of them safe, and being needed is not the same as being loved.

A character-driven story of military action, trauma, desire, and unhealthy attachment.

## Torn Usunder — Alternate Universes

The bookshelf also includes non-canon alternate-universe (AU) stories for Torn Usunder.

These stories explore different circumstances, possibilities, and versions of the characters outside the main continuity.

- AU events do not establish facts about the main story.
- Character histories, relationships, settings, and outcomes may differ.
- Details from one AU should not be assumed to apply to another.
- Check each story's introduction for its premise and content notices.

They are separate possibilities, not required reading for the main story.

## Exploring the Bookshelf

Start with the site's homepage, then choose a story.

Depending on the project, you may find:

- **Chapters and stories** — Narrative fiction, including drafts and works in progress.
- **Characters** — Profiles, relationships, and background information.
- **Worldbuilding** — Settings, societies, history, technology, and power systems.
- **Planning notes** — Outlines, scene ideas, themes, and possible directions.
- **Alternate universes** — Stories and notes outside the main continuity.

Not every project uses the same structure or has the same amount of material available.

## Drafts, Continuity, and Spoilers

This is a living collection, not a finished reference book.

A planning note is not necessarily an established story fact. Drafts may contain ideas that are later revised, and archived material may no longer apply. Follow any status or continuity labels provided on individual pages.

Character profiles, outlines, and worldbuilding notes may reveal developments beyond the published chapters. If you prefer to avoid spoilers, begin with the narrative and consult supporting material afterward.

## Content Notice

These projects include violence, trauma, and mature themes. Individual stories may contain additional material, including graphic injury, coercive or unhealthy dynamics, and other distressing content.

Read each story's content notices before starting. Alternate-universe stories may have different notices from the main continuity.

Depiction is not endorsement.
